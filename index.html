How NeedLoop works

Designed to keep the first step easy.

1

Say what you need

Describe the item, your budget, and your general area. No complicated listing required.

2

Get potential matches

Sellers can look through requests and decide whether they have something that fits.

3

Connect safely

Discuss details and arrange a safe transaction. This demo doesn't send messages or process payments.

NeedLoop is an early prototype. Sample requests are fictional. Requests you add are saved only in this browser on this device; other visitors cannot see them yet. This demo does not have accounts, real messaging, identity verification, or payments. Meet in public, protect your personal information, and never send money before verifying an item.
